## 🎥 About Teleprompter

**Teleprompter — For Creators** is a mobile teleprompter designed to make recording videos easier, smoother, and more natural.

Keep your script in front of you while recording, adjust the scrolling speed to your speaking pace, and customize the reading layout to fit the way you read.

### Built for

* 🎥 Content creators
* 📱 YouTubers & short-form creators
* 🎙️ Podcasters & presenters
* 💼 Professionals recording presentations
* 📚 Students & educators
* 🗣️ Anyone who needs to speak naturally on camera

---

## ✨ Features

### 📝 Distraction-free reading

Keep your script visible while recording without constantly looking away from the camera.

### ⚡ Smooth scrolling

Adjust the scrolling speed to match your natural speaking pace.

### 📐 Flexible reading layouts

Choose how much text you want visible on screen:

| Mode           | Description                                            |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Narrow**     | Only a few words per line for easier reading           |
| **Standard**   | A balanced reading experience                          |
| **Full Width** | Uses the available screen width for maximum visibility |

### 🎛️ Customizable reading experience

Adjust the teleprompter to match your workflow instead of adapting your workflow to the app.

### 🎨 Creator-focused UI

A clean interface designed to keep the focus on your script and camera rather than the controls.
